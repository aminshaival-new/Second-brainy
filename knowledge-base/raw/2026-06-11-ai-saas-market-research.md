# AI SaaS Market Research — June 2026

Source: Web research via WebSearch, June 2026

## Market Size
- Global SaaS market projected $375–465B by 2026
- AI segment within SaaS growing at ~38% CAGR
- Global AI software revenue reached $118.6B in 2025
- Vertical SaaS outpacing horizontal tools by 2x

## Pricing Model Evolution (2026)
- "AI as a feature" era is over — age of AI-native apps has arrived
- 61% of SaaS companies now using hybrid pricing models
- Hybrid = base subscription + variable usage tiers
- Gartner projects 40% of enterprise contracts will include outcome-based pricing by end of 2026
- Credit-based systems (e.g. Lovable) becoming standard
- Shift from per-seat → usage-based → outcome/value-based

## Business Model Shifts
- Agentic AI pricing is the new frontier — agents billed by task completion
- Pricing increasingly treated as a strategic product capability, not a finance decision
- Vertical SaaS (industry-specific) growing 2x faster than horizontal tools
