# AI Automation Tools & Workflows — Research June 2026

Source: Web research via WebSearch, June 2026

## The Big Three Platforms

### Zapier
- 7,000+ app integrations — broadest coverage
- Zapier AI Agents out of beta — agents plan their own steps vs rigid trigger→action
- Best for: non-technical teams, broad app coverage, simple lead routing
- Pricing: higher per-task cost at volume

### Make (formerly Integromat)
- 2,000+ integrations, 9,000+ pre-built solutions
- Visual drag-and-drop canvas builder
- Maia AI assistant builds scenarios from natural language
- Make AI Agents for autonomous task execution
- Best for: complex data workflows, visual building, complex lead scoring

### n8n
- Open-source, self-hostable (or cloud)
- 70+ native AI nodes, LangChain integration (v2.0)
- Maximum flexibility for custom agent building
- Best for: technical teams, complex custom systems, cost efficiency
- Cost advantage: 80–90% cheaper than Zapier for complex high-volume workflows

## Common Use Cases in 2026
- Lead scoring and routing automation
- Onboarding campaign automation
- CRM sync and enrichment
- AI-powered customer support workflows
- Content publishing pipelines
- Invoice/billing automation
- Internal notification systems

## Market Trend
- Shift from "Trigger → Action" to autonomous AI agents that plan steps
- All three platforms have launched native AI agent capabilities in 2025–2026
