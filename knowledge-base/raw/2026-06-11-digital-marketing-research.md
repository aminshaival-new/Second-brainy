# Digital Marketing for AI/SaaS — Research June 2026

Source: Web research via WebSearch, June 2026

## Top Strategies for AI/SaaS Companies in 2026

### Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
- Biggest shift in SaaS SEO — optimizing for AI assistants, not just search engines
- ChatGPT dominates with ~78% of AI traffic referrals
- Customers ask AI for software recommendations — you must appear in those answers
- Requires clear, structured, authoritative content that LLMs cite

### AI-Driven Marketing Automation
- Leading SaaS companies run large portions of marketing with minimal human intervention
- AI agents manage: onboarding campaigns, lifecycle emails, pipeline scoring, partner activation
- Massive leverage for lean teams

### Product-Led Growth (PLG)
- PLG is table stakes in 2026, not a differentiator
- Winners pair PLG with sales-assisted intelligence + brand-led storytelling

### Ecosystem-Led Growth
- As paid acquisition costs rise, companies shift to ecosystem/partnership models
- Partnerships deliver higher-intent leads at lower CAC than direct channels

### Trust as Differentiator
- Data privacy, security, compliance communications win enterprise buyers
- Especially important for AI companies handling customer data

### Personalization at Scale
- AI enables 1:1 personalization across website, email, ads, in-app messaging
