# Digital Marketing

> Strategies for growing an AI or SaaS company through digital channels — what works in 2026.

---

## The Biggest Shift: Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Traditional SEO optimizes for Google search rankings. AEO optimizes for being cited by AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini).

- ChatGPT alone drives ~78% of AI assistant traffic referrals
- Customers increasingly ask AI "what's the best tool for X?" — you must appear in those answers
- Requires clear, structured, authoritative content that LLMs can parse and cite
- Think: comprehensive topic pages, clear positioning statements, well-structured FAQs

This is directly relevant to [[brand-building]] — brands with clear signals get cited by AI systems.

## Core Channel Playbook for AI/SaaS

### Product-Led Growth (PLG)
Table stakes in 2026 — not a differentiator on its own. Free tiers, trials, and self-serve onboarding are expected. Win by pairing PLG with:
- Sales-assisted intelligence (knowing when to reach out)
- Brand-led storytelling

### Ecosystem-Led Growth
Paid acquisition costs are rising. Partnerships now deliver higher-intent leads at lower CAC than direct channels. Build integrations, co-marketing deals, and referral networks.

### AI-Driven Marketing Automation
Top teams run marketing with minimal daily human intervention:
- AI agents manage onboarding sequences, lifecycle emails, pipeline scoring
- Tools: [[ai-automation]] (n8n, Make, Zapier) + your CRM + AI models
- Massive leverage for lean teams

### Content & SEO
- Long-form, expert content still works — but must be optimized for AI citation, not just ranking
- Thought leadership from founders drives trust and brand authority
- Video and short-form social increasingly AI-generated

### Trust Marketing
Especially for AI companies: explicitly communicate security, privacy, compliance, and data handling. Enterprise buyers require it. See [[brand-building]].

## Personalization at Scale
AI enables true 1:1 personalization across:
- Website copy (dynamic by segment)
- Email sequences
- Ad creative
- In-app messaging

## Key Metrics
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) by channel
- LTV:CAC ratio (target ≥3:1)
- Conversion rates: visitor → trial → paid
- NPS and referral rate (brand health)
