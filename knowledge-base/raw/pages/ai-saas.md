# AI SaaS

> The business of selling AI-powered software as a subscription service — market dynamics, pricing models, and growth patterns as of 2026.

---

## Market Size & Growth

- Global SaaS market: $375–465B projected by end of 2026
- AI segment within SaaS growing at ~38% CAGR
- Global AI software revenue: $118.6B in 2025
- **Vertical SaaS** (industry-specific tools) growing 2x faster than horizontal tools

## The Pricing Revolution

The old model (per-seat flat subscription) is being replaced by:

| Model | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Hybrid** | Base fee + usage tiers | Most AI SaaS (61% now use this) |
| **Usage-based** | Pay per API call / token / action | Developer tools, APIs |
| **Outcome-based** | Pay per result/task completed | Agentic AI products |
| **Credit wallets** | Pre-purchase credits, spend as you go | Consumer + prosumer AI tools |

Gartner projects 40% of enterprise contracts will include outcome-based components by end of 2026. Pricing is now treated as a product capability, not a finance decision.

## The Agentic Shift

The "AI as a feature" era is over. 2026 marks the clear arrival of **AI-native apps** — where AI is the product, not a feature bolted on. Key implications:
- Products are billed by what they *accomplish*, not what they *provide*
- Agentic workflows (multi-step autonomous AI) are the new battleground
- Customers expect measurable outcomes, not just capabilities
