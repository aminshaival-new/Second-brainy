# AI Automation

> Building and selling AI-powered workflow automation — tools, platforms, use cases, and what clients actually need in 2026.

---

## What AI Automation Is

AI automation connects apps, data, and AI models into workflows that run without human intervention. In 2026 this has evolved from simple "if this then that" rules to **autonomous AI agents** that plan their own steps to complete a goal.

## The Big Three Platforms

### Zapier
- **Best for:** Non-technical clients, broad app coverage (7,000+ integrations)
- AI Agents out of beta — can plan steps autonomously vs rigid trigger→action
- Highest cost at volume, but lowest barrier to entry
- Good for: lead routing, CRM sync, notification workflows

### Make (formerly Integromat)
- **Best for:** Complex data workflows, visual builders
- Maia AI assistant builds scenarios from natural language descriptions
- Make AI Agents for autonomous task execution
- 2,000+ integrations, 9,000+ pre-built solutions
- Good for: complex lead scoring, multi-step data pipelines

### n8n
- **Best for:** Technical teams, custom agent building, cost efficiency
- Open-source (self-host or cloud)
- 70+ native AI nodes, native LangChain integration (v2.0)
- 80–90% cheaper than Zapier for complex high-volume workflows
- Good for: fully custom AI agents, maximum flexibility

## Common Client Use Cases

| Use case | Best platform |
|---|---|
| Simple lead routing + CRM sync | Zapier |
| Complex lead scoring + nurture | Make |
| Custom AI agent + full control | n8n |
| Marketing email automation | Any (integrate with Klaviyo/ActiveCampaign) |
| Content publishing pipelines | n8n or Make |
| Invoice/billing automation | Zapier or Make |
| AI customer support workflows | n8n (custom LLM integration) |

## The Shift: Rules → Agents

2026 marker: all three platforms launched native AI agent capabilities. Clients now want agents that:
- Respond to unstructured inputs (emails, Slack messages, form submissions)
- Make decisions based on context (not just routing rules)
- Execute multi-step plans autonomously

This is a significant upsell opportunity: migrate clients from basic Zaps → intelligent agents.

## Selling AI Automation Services

Key client pain points to lead with:
- "You're paying staff to do repetitive tasks that AI can handle"
- "Your leads are falling through the cracks between tools"
- "Your team spends X hours/week on Y — we can automate that"

Pricing models for automation services:
- One-time build fee + monthly retainer for maintenance
- Usage-based (% of cost savings delivered)
- Outcome-based (per lead processed, per hour saved)
