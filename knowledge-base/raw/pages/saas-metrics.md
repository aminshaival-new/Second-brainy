# SaaS Metrics

> The essential numbers for running and evaluating a SaaS business — definitions, targets, and what to track.

---

## Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Healthy target |
|---|---|---|
| **MRR** | Monthly Recurring Revenue — total subscription revenue per month | Growing consistently |
| **ARR** | Annual Recurring Revenue — MRR × 12 | ≥$1M = meaningful scale |
| **NRR** | Net Revenue Retention — revenue from existing customers after churn + expansion | >100% = growing without new customers |
| **Expansion MRR** | Revenue added from upgrades/upsells to existing customers | Should offset or exceed churn |

## Acquisition Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Healthy target |
|---|---|---|
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost — total sales+marketing spend ÷ new customers | Varies by segment |
| **LTV** | Lifetime Value — avg revenue per customer over their lifetime | LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1 |
| **Payback period** | Months to recoup CAC from a customer | <12 months for SMB, <18 for mid-market |

## Retention Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Healthy target |
|---|---|---|
| **Churn rate** | % of customers (or revenue) lost per month | <2% monthly for SMB, <1% enterprise |
| **Logo churn** | Number of customers lost | Track separately from revenue churn |
| **Revenue churn** | % of MRR lost | More important than logo churn |

## Growth Metrics

- **MoM growth rate:** target 10–15%+ for early-stage
- **Rule of 40:** growth rate + profit margin should be ≥40% for healthy SaaS
- **DAU/MAU ratio (stickiness):** for usage-based products

## Pricing Model Impact on Metrics
With outcome-based / usage-based pricing (see [[ai-saas]]):
- NRR can exceed 130%+ if usage grows naturally
- Churn is less binary — customers may reduce usage rather than fully cancel
- CAC stays same but LTV can grow much faster
